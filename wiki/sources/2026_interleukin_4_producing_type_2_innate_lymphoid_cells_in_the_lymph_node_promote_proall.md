# Interleukin-4-producing type 2 innate lymphoid cells in the lymph node promote proallergic Tfh13 cell differentiation

## Citation

- Verified title: Interleukin-4-producing type 2 innate lymphoid cells in the lymph node promote proallergic Tfh13 cell differentiation
- Authors: DongUk Lee, Meng-Ping Lu, Priya Rajamanimuthu, Mrinmoy Das, Enxhi Ferraj, Courtney Fisher, Peri Matatia, Chun-Wei Chen, Raif S. Geha, Caroline L. Sokol, and Uthaman Gowthaman
- Journal: Immunity
- Publication year: 2026
- Issue date shown in PDF: August 11, 2026
- DOI: 10.1016/j.immuni.2026.04.015
- Metadata source: local PDF first pages and article text

## Source Type

- primary mechanistic immunology study
- main cell focus: ILC2
- adaptive partner: Tfh13 cells, with downstream high-affinity IgE and anaphylaxis readouts
- compartment focus: lung-draining lymph node after intranasal allergen sensitization

## Evidence Profile

- Overall confidence: high for the source-specific mouse claim that lymph-node ILC2s promote Tfh13 differentiation through ILC2-derived IL-4 and T-cell IL-4Ralpha signaling.
- Species: mouse. Human allergy literature is discussed in the article background, but this source should not be cited as direct human lung or human lymph-node evidence.
- Tissue / compartment: airway allergen exposure, lung-draining lymph node, lung ILC2 comparison, and skin-draining lymph node comparison.
- Disease anchor: allergic sensitization, pathogenic IgE, passive cutaneous anaphylaxis, and active anaphylaxis models.
- Major perturbations: Nmur1Cre Gata3fl/fl and RoralphaCre Gata3fl/fl ILC2-depletion logic, mixed bone marrow chimeras for ILC2-derived IL-4, T-cell-specific IL-4Ralpha deletion, exogenous IL-4 rescue, CCR8 blockade/deficiency/chimeras, and ILC2-specific MHCII deletion.
- Assays: flow cytometry, cytokine reporter mice, immunofluorescence, adoptive transfer, mixed bone marrow chimeras, ELISA, PCA, active anaphylaxis, and in vitro migration/co-culture assays.

## Why It Matters Here

This source adds a lymph-node adaptive-immunity branch to the ILC2 map. It shows that after airway allergen sensitization, ILC2s can move into draining lymph nodes and provide an early IL-4 signal that selectively supports Tfh13 differentiation, high-affinity IgE, and anaphylaxis-associated outputs. It should sit beside lung ILC2 OX40L, PD-L1, and Gata3high Treg evidence, while staying clearly labeled as draining-lymph-node mouse evidence.

## Key Findings

- Intranasal Alternaria plus NP-OVA sensitization produced stronger lung-draining lymph-node Tfh13 responses and high-affinity IgE/anaphylaxis readouts than the intradermal route in the reported mouse system.
- ILC2s accumulated in lung-draining lymph nodes before the emergence of Tfh13 cells; the source describes GATA3+ ICOS+ and KLRG1+ activated ILC2s positioned near Tfh-B-cell interaction regions.
- ILC2-depletion approaches using Nmur1Cre Gata3fl/fl and an orthogonal RoralphaCre Gata3fl/fl model strongly reduced Tfh13 generation and high-affinity IgE/anaphylaxis-associated outputs, while total Tfh, Tfh2, and many Th2 outputs were comparatively preserved.
- Lung-draining lymph-node ILC2s showed higher IL-4 and MHCII expression than lung ILC2s after allergen exposure, but ILC2 MHCII was dispensable for Tfh13 generation in vivo.
- Mixed bone marrow chimeras supported a specific requirement for ILC2-derived IL-4 in Tfh13, but not Tfh2, differentiation.
- T-cell-intrinsic IL-4Ralpha signaling was required for Tfh13 generation, and exogenous IL-4 could restore Tfh13 responses in ILC2-deficient mice.
- CCR8 promoted ILC2 trafficking into draining lymph nodes; CCR8 perturbation impaired lymph-node ILC2 recruitment and reduced Tfh13 generation.

## Claim-Level Confidence

- High confidence:
  in the reported mouse allergen-sensitization model, lymph-node ILC2s are upstream regulators of Tfh13 differentiation.
- High confidence:
  ILC2-derived IL-4 and T-cell IL-4Ralpha signaling are required for the Tfh13 branch tested in this source.
- High confidence:
  CCR8 contributes to ILC2 migration into draining lymph nodes and thereby supports the Tfh13 response in this system.
- Medium-high confidence:
  airway allergen exposure can couple lung-draining lymph-node ILC2 behavior to pathogenic IgE biology, but the claim should remain model- and route-labeled.
- Low confidence:
  this source alone does not prove that the same ILC2-Tfh13 mechanism operates in human asthma, human lung tissue, BAL, sputum, or all forms of allergic disease.

## Methods and Context

- All durable mechanistic claims above are from mouse experiments in allergen-sensitization systems.
- The main immunization context was intranasal Alternaria plus NP-OVA, with comparisons to intradermal exposure.
- The source compares lung ILC2s and lung-draining lymph-node ILC2s, but the central adaptive mechanism is in draining lymph nodes rather than lung parenchyma.
- The authors used two ILC2-depletion strategies and discuss Cre-line caveats, including RoralphaCre activity in peripheral lung CD4 T cells.
- MHCII-positive ILC2s could promote T-cell proliferation in vitro when peptide-pulsed, but ILC2 MHCII loss did not reduce Tfh13 generation in vivo.

## Caveats

- Do not summarize this as a generic "ILC2s activate adaptive immunity" claim. The source specifically supports an ILC2-IL-4-Tfh13 axis in draining lymph nodes after airway allergen exposure.
- Do not convert this into direct human asthma, BAL, sputum, or bronchial-biopsy evidence.
- Do not equate Tfh13 support with broad Tfh2 support; the paper emphasizes distinct requirements for Tfh13 versus Tfh2 differentiation.
- Keep the CCR8 branch separate from previously ingested lung-tissue CCR8-CCL8 ILC2 motility evidence; here the source-specific output is lymph-node trafficking and Tfh13 generation.

## Contradiction and Supersession

- Supersession status:
