    Integration

    • ILC2 entity, regulatory mechanisms and pulmonary-disease topic: redox capacity as a requirement for pathogenic lipid handling; preserved Red5/Th2, co-treatment timing and human-translation limitations.
    • ILC2 entity and regulatory mechanisms: pancreatic progenitor niche as a tissue-labeled comparator, without importing pancreatic lineage relationships into lung.
    • ILC3 entity and regulatory mechanisms: gut chemokine recruitment restraint, separated from newborn pulmonary IGF1 developmental support.
    • Core evidence digest: source-bounded comparison of metabolic survival and reciprocal niche regulation.
    • Homepage and reference registries: three new entries; no unrelated wiki corpus included.

    Independent Review

    • Round 1: three source notes and all seven integration/homepage diffs reviewed against supplied evidence and existing ILC context. No blocker identified. One major wording issue: ILC2-dependent abundance and Dpp4-lineage contribution were conflated in an entity-page sentence.
    • Correction: explicitly separated the abundance perturbation result from the independent lineage-tracing result; no claim that ILC2s directly determine fate allocation remains.
    • Round 2: reviewer inspected the corrected entity sentence and reused the prior full-source review. Sign-off: no blocking accuracy issues remain.
    • Remaining caveats: Science supplementary panels not directly inspected; human associations/cultures are not clinical efficacy; pancreatic/gut findings are not direct pulmonary causality; ASCT2 specificity, macrophage phagocytosis, and OSM/LIF necessity remain bounded.

    Public Export Policy

    The public copy contains explanatory notes and citations, not source PDFs or extracted article text. Existing repository metadata and deployment workflow are preserved. Exporting the local upload-ready directory does not commit, push, or deploy the repository.
